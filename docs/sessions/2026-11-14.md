## Findings and process notes

- **Stack e2e not run locally for #161.** It failed in CI: a stack test expected a new personal feed to be fetched at once, and F-31 changes that on purpose. The test now checks the hour and then makes the feed due. Lesson: when a change alters behaviour, run `scripts/stack-e2e.sh` locally, not only the smoke test.
- **The namespace test left listener processes behind.** `$!` of a shell function was the subshell, not the listener. It also reused a variable name in the DNS listener. Both are fixed: the listeners are started with `ip netns exec`, and they have a five-minute guard. The leftovers were stopped by PID. After every run, processes, namespaces, `/etc/netns` and the host's `iptables -S` were checked.
- **A commit went in with two unit tests failing.** A pipe hid the exit status. It was fixed in the next commit, before any push.
- **The disk reached 95%.** The smoke test's `status.sh` line failed. 7.9 GB of unused build cache was pruned.
- **Still on the host, waiting for the maintainer's decision:** 640 anonymous, unattached PostgreSQL volumes (26.4 GB) and a 12 GB `.turbo` cache of another project. Nothing of other projects was removed.
- **Two named mutations of acceptance criterion 2 were not itemized before.** Both are now shown: restore without the roles, and the interactions cascade.
