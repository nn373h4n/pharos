## Decisions

- Personal RSS: 5 feeds per person, interval at least 60 minutes, owner-only; switched by `PHAROS_PERSONAL_FEEDS` (`on` or `off`).
- Test fetch results travel from the worker to the API through Redis under the worker's own prefix, read as untrusted data (ADR-0013, `Proposed`).
- A worker reads the account status of a feed's owner, so disabled people's feeds are not fetched (F-32, ADR-0014, `Proposed`).
- The test-only address allowance (the fake feed host) is read only with `PHAROS_ENVIRONMENT=test`, and only the stack-e2e overlay sets it.
- The starter catalogue (25 sources: 16 feeds, 7 GitHub repositories, 2 Hacker News searches) was approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-30 and is imported switched off.
- Choices made without the maintainer that should be reviewed: F-31 (the first fetch of a new personal feed is due at once, bounded by 10 new addresses an hour); the Hacker News search `llm self-hosted` was tried only against the search API, not through the connector.
